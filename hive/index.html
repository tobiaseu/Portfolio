Hive.

The tractor is the body, the hive is the network

A cockpit for Level 3 autonomy in the orchard.

While the tractor keeps the row on its own, the operator, even a seasonal worker on day one, can plan the day, tag the field and take over safely when a person steps into the row.

RoleResearch, UX/UI, prototypeTeam5 peopleTime3 monthsYear2026
01 · Inside the cab

Two pads, one knob.

A detachable horizontal dashboard for control, a vertical screen on the pillar for navigation, and a rotary knob under the right hand.

03 · Multi-fleet

One farm, many tractors.

A hardware-agnostic kit moves across a mixed fleet. Every machine shares its job, progress and take-over state, so the crew sees who needs a hand.

Touch to plan, a knob for the field.

06 · Managing the jobs

The day, at a glance.

Today's jobs carry a colour and an AUTO or MANUAL tag. Zoom out and the same language plans the whole week, with a line for "now".

Outcome

A modular kit, not a new fleet.

Expert reviewers praised the clarity of the layout and a kit that protects what farmers already own. Next: a quick-message channel so crews can coordinate shared tasks.
