About

Hi, I'm Tobia.

Industrial product designer with an interdisciplinary, experimental approach: from products and exhibitions to UX/UI.

I approach design in an interdisciplinary and experimental way, embracing diverse methods to grow both creatively and professionally.

I hold a degree in Industrial Product Design, with a passion for projects in all their forms, from product development to visual communication. My background includes exhibition design, prototyping and interactive design, while my most recent work has focused on UX and UI.

I am ambitious, with strong interpersonal skills, and I often act as a cohesive force within teams.
