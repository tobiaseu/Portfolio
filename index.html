Tier 1 Selected work — designed and built end to end
01
Fanta.me
2026 — Personal Project Individual Project Design + Build
Mobile App UX/UI Design Game Design
The fantasy game of real life. Friends open a room for a trip, the office or a wedding, build a deck of bonus and malus cards, then call and vote each other's moments live.
  • Two worlds. A calm lobby with no navigation, and an in-game dashboard with five tabs: Dashboard, Deck, Live, Standings, Profile.
  • Social mechanics. Collectible card deck, group voting by majority, one-shot fantapowers and a team podium that updates live.
  • Real product, not a mockup. Designed in Figma and built as a working Expo (React Native) app in TypeScript, refined over 20+ review rounds including a Nielsen heuristic evaluation.

02
Iprefab
2025–2026 — Internship Case Study Iprefab OY, Finland Concept + Build
UX/UI Design AI Configurator Web Design
A concept for Iprefab, an aggregator of Finnish prefab builders. An AI guide helps people shape their ideal house across suppliers, then connects them with the right builder.
  • Design first, then the builder. A 3D wireframe house that rebuilds and rotates with every choice, guided by a natural-language prompt.
  • Trust through clarity. Six calm phases, a live price estimate and model comparisons, ending with images to download and a meeting request.
  • Nordic quiet. Deep blue, glass cards and thin lines, bilingual Finnish and English. Built with Next.js and Tailwind. Portfolio case study, not the official product.
